# How to use Sec-Pulse

Your daily cyber security news and CySA+ study app. Everything is saved on this phone only, so export a backup now and then (Settings → Your progress).

## Every morning
- **7:25am notification**: your top 5 stories, overnight stats, today's lesson and the question of the day. GitHub sometimes sends it a bit late.
- **Tap it** to open **This morning's brief**: the same stories with full summaries and links, plus the lesson and question. It's also on the Brief tab ("📰 This morning's brief") for the rest of the day.
- Long-press the notification to read more of it without opening the app.
- **Sunday 6pm**: the weekly digest, with the week's top 10 stories and that week's lessons.

## Brief tab
- **Streak, cards due, lessons done** at the top. 🧊 next to the streak means you hold a streak freeze.
- **Rank card**: your level and XP. Tap it for your **Analyst profile**: skills, achievements, a calendar of the days you studied, and your quiz accuracy over time.
- **Daily quests**: four optional goals that reset at midnight. Finish all four to earn a 🧊 streak freeze, which saves your streak if you miss a single day.
- **Exam countdown**: set your exam date in Settings to get days left and a daily target.
- **Focus area**: your weakest exam domain. Tap **Drill 10** for questions from it, with past misses first.
- **Top for you**, **today's lesson** and the **question of the day**.

## News tab
- **Filters**: For you, Latest, Australia, Exploited, Real estate & BEC, Microsoft 365, Later, Saved.
- **Tap a headline** to read the story. Read stories are dimmed.
- **☆** saves a story (you can add a note to saved ones). The **clock** adds it to Read later.
- **score N**: tap to see why a story ranked and to **mute** a source or tag.
- **CVE-… ↗** links open the vulnerability on NVD. **Fix by** shows CISA's patch deadline for actively exploited flaws (red when it's close).
- **Pull down** at the top to refresh. News updates every 3 hours.

## Learn tab
- **Lessons** for all four CySA+ domains. Each has key points, an exam tip, an on-the-job note, a video search link and a 3-question quiz.
- **Quick quiz** (10 questions) and **Practice exam** (30 questions weighted like the real exam, with a rough scaled score).
- **Timed practice exam**: 30 questions in 58 minutes, the real exam's pace. Closing it cancels the attempt.
- **Review mistakes**: questions you've got wrong, until you get them right more often than wrong.
- **PBQ practice**: hands-on scenarios with logs, headers and scan output.

## Cards tab
- Multiple-choice flashcards with spaced repetition: right answers come back later, wrong ones come back sooner. 8 new cards a day.
- **Review missed cards**, **Acronym drill** (EDR, TTP, TLP, SCAP…) and **Cram 10** don't change your schedule.

## Levelling up
- Everything earns XP: answers, cards, lessons, quiz rounds, reading news, quests and achievements.
- Each question and card earns XP once a day, so repeats are practice, not farming.
- Ranks go from Trainee Analyst to Cyber Legend (level 50).

## Settings (⚙️)
- **What's new**, **Roadmap** and this guide.
- **Appearance**: System, Dark or Light.
- **Exam date**, **Udemy link**, **muted sources and tags**.
- **Notifications on this phone**: turn on, copy the code for GitHub, test. If you reinstall the app, copy the new code into GitHub again.
- **Feed health**, **Export / Import backup** and **Reset**.

## If something's not right
- **No notification**: check the run at GitHub → Sec-Pulse → Actions → Morning brief. "subscription expired" means turn notifications on again in Settings and update the code in GitHub.
- **Old version showing**: close the app fully (swipe it away) and reopen it.
- **News looks stale**: tap ↻. It shows when the next update is due.
- **Lost progress**: Settings → Import backup with your latest export.
